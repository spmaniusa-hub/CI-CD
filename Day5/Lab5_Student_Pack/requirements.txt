Inventory reservation requirements

1. A positive request must succeed when requested quantity is less than OR EQUAL TO free stock.
2. A request greater than free stock must return False and must not change state.
3. Zero or negative requests must raise ValueError.
4. Reserved stock must never exceed available stock.
5. Release quantity must be positive and cannot exceed the currently reserved quantity.
6. Business rule not obvious from the implementation: reservations that consume all free stock are allowed, but the SKU must then be reported as out_of_stock in any generated summary.
